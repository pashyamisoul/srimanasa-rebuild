About Srimanasa

More than advice: complete financial stewardship

Founded in 2012 on a solid, structural investment philosophy, Srimanasa is built for long-term investors and growing businesses alike. Our name reflects our belief: your money deserves a guardian, not just a broker. We believe the size of your investment should never affect the quality of guidance you receive; everyone deserves the very best.

Our Vision

Financial markets should be equitable, accessible and efficient, so that every investor, large or small, can earn a fair and honest return.

Our Mission

To develop wealth through systematic, forward-thinking concepts, powered by qualified professionals and a strong, dependable network around every client.

Our Promise

We don't merely help you maximise profits. We look after your entire situation, protecting your assets while building competitive, sustainable returns.

What we do

Seven services, one trusted partner

Whether you're an individual investor or running payroll for a growing team, we cover the full spectrum, so you never have to juggle multiple firms.

Investment Consulting

Guidance for asset owners, fund managers and trustees on funds, asset classes and portfolio construction, built around a clear allocation strategy.

Payroll Processing

End-to-end payroll set up for your business: wage changes, attendance, deductions and accurate net-pay, handled across departments.

EPF Support

Complete support with the Employees' Provident Fund: registration, contributions and compliance with one of the world's largest social-security bodies.

ESIC Assistance

Setting up and resolving ESIC matters, covering medical care, financial benefits and maternity support for your workforce.

Accounting

Bookkeeping for general ledgers, maintenance of fixed deposits and accounts, plus annual financial compilation and evaluation.

GST Services

A complete GST platform, helping individuals and businesses register, file and stay compliant as they start and grow.

Income Tax

Filing, tax planning and structuring, property & real-estate taxation, cross-border taxation and indirect taxation, done right, on time.
